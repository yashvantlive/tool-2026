Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Developer Account Converter?

It is a professional optimizer designed specifically for Google Play Console developer accounts. It converts and resizes images to meet the exact dimensions and file size limits of the Developer Header (4096×2304) and Developer Icon (512×512).

What are the exact requirements for Google Play Developer Headers and Icons?

The Developer Header must be exactly 4096×2304 px, and the Developer Icon must be exactly 512×512 px. Both must be JPG or 24-bit PNG (without transparency or alpha channels) and must not exceed the 1 MB file size limit.

Why does this tool force JPG format?

Google Play Console requires headers and developer icons to have no transparency (24-bit). Forcing JPG format automatically removes alpha channels (transparency) by rendering them over a clean solid white background, preventing console upload rejection errors.

Can I crop my image to fit the 16:9 ratio?

Yes, the built-in crop editor has preset aspects like 16:9 (Header) and 1:1 (Icon) to help you perfectly crop and center your branding before conversion.
