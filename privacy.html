Privacy Policy

Your Privacy is Our Priority

🔒 Data Storage Policy

We DO NOT store any of your data.

All image processing, conversion, and optimization happens entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your images never leave your device.

🖥️ Browser-Native Processing

This tool uses:

  • Canvas API - For image processing
  • File API - For reading uploaded files
  • Local Storage - Only for user preferences (optional)

No server uploads. No cloud storage. No third-party tracking.

🌐 Third-Party Services

We use the following external resources:

  • Google Fonts - For typography (Outfit font family)
  • CDN Libraries - JSZip, Cropper.js (loaded from public CDNs)

These services may have their own privacy policies.

🍪 Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking or analytics. Any browser storage used is solely for:

  • Saving your quality/dimension preferences
  • Remembering your last used settings

You can clear this data anytime from your browser settings.

For privacy-related questions, please contact us through YouLearn.community.
